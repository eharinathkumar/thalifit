Your data stays yours.

On-device by default

Your profile, food log, activity, weight, water, and health readings are stored in this browser on this device.

AI requests

When you deliberately use an AI feature, only the information needed for that request is sent to the configured Thalify AI service. Health readings are not sent to AI.

Backups

You control exported backup files. Keep them somewhere private and import them only on devices you trust.

Thalify provides estimates and food coaching, not medical advice.
